# Data licensing notes

The repository-level MIT license applies to original code and original project material.

It does **not** override licenses or terms attached to external source data.

- CBS-derived name lists used through `alumag/hebrew-names`: follow the source repository's data notice.
- Wikidata structured data: CC0.
- Wiktionary/Kaikki-derived material: attribution/share-alike obligations may apply.
- DAHPN: used for research verification only unless a reuse license is confirmed.

The current sample meanings are short original Hebrew paraphrases of linguistic facts and are marked `draft` until reviewed.
